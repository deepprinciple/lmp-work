OPLS-AA parameters for 2,2,2-trifluorethanol were reported in an Appendix to
the thesis of Erin Duffy at Yale in 1994, 
but were not reported elsewhere. The parameters are included in the OPLS-AA 
parameter files that we distribute. The parameters were tested by Erin in 
simulations of liquid TFE at 25 C. They give a delHvap of 10.7 kcal/mol
and density of 1.39 g/cm3 (exptl is 10.41 and 1.38).
